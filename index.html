almanac.

Privacy Policy — last updated September 24, 2026

almanac. is a watch face for Wear OS. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and why.

What almanac. accesses

To display its face, and to provide its features, almanac. requires the following:

  • Step count (via Android Health Connect). Used to render the step activity indicator. Read-only; never transmitted, shared, or stored outside your device.
  • Battery level. Used to render the battery indicator. Never transmitted, shared, or stored outside your device.
  • Weather conditions. Provided by the Wear OS platform's own weather data source and shown in the forecast strip. almanac. does not fetch weather data itself and does not collect or store it.

What almanac. does not do

  • almanac. makes no network requests of its own. It has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.
  • almanac. does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not use crash-reporting services.
  • almanac. does not require an account or login.
  • almanac. does not access your contacts, messages, photos, or location.

Data storage

Your preferences (light or dark mode, and your chosen time and date formats) are stored locally in almanac.'s private app storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere. All step count, battery, and weather data described above is read in memory for display purposes only and is never persisted to disk.

Uninstalling almanac. removes all of its stored data from your device.

Changes to this policy

almanac. is under active development. As features are added, this policy will be updated to accurately reflect what the app accesses and why. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "last updated" date above.
