Privacy Policy — E-Ink Watch Face

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This watch face reads the following data directly from your device, solely to display it on the watch face itself:

  • Step count (via Android Health Connect) — used to render the step activity indicator. Read-only; never transmitted, shared, or stored outside your device.
  • Battery level — used to render the battery indicator. Never transmitted, shared, or stored outside your device.
  • Weather conditions — provided by the Wear OS platform's own weather data source. This app does not fetch weather data itself and does not collect or store it.

This app does not use the internet, does not collect analytics, does not display ads, does not require an account or login, and does not share any data with third parties. All data described above is read in memory for display purposes only and is never persisted to disk or transmitted anywhere.

Customization

This watch face offers on-device display customization, including a light color mode and formatting options for the time and date. These settings only change how information is drawn on the watch face; they do not add any new data collection, and your chosen settings are stored only on your device.
